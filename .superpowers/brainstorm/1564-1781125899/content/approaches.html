Three Approaches

Which direction fits best? Click your pick.

A

Quick Wins — Polish What's There

Focus on the 3 highest-impact changes: gallery category filtering, a CV/resume page, and a better home page with featured work. Fast to build, immediately professional.

Pros

  • Fast to ship
  • Low risk
  • Clearly improves the weak spots

Cons

  • Doesn't show process
  • Similar to most portfolios
B ⭐ Recommended

Process-First — Show How You Think

Build gallery filtering + 2–3 case study pages showing sketches → finals, plus a CV page. The process pages are what game studios and agencies actually remember.

Pros

  • Strong differentiator
  • Appeals to both game studios & agencies
  • WIP material already exists

Cons

  • More work to set up
  • Requires curating content per project
C

Full Suite — All the Features

Everything: filtering, case studies, CV page, skills visualization, contact/inquiry form, and a featured work hero on the home page.

Pros

  • Most complete portfolio
  • Covers every recruiter touchpoint

Cons

  • Significant build time
  • Risk of doing many things averagely instead of a few things well

My recommendation: B — process pages are rare and remembered. Answer in terminal too.
